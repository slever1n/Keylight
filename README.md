## ?? Features

### 1. Color Temperature Spectrum (Kelvin Scale)
- **Continuous Range**: Smooth slider from **2000K (Deep Warm Amber / Candlelight)** up to **9500K (Arctic Cold Daylight)**.
- **Accurate Blackbody Physics**: Converts Planckian locus color temperatures to sRGB for true studio-grade illumination.
- **Quick-Tap Presets**:
  - ??? **2000K** — Candlelight & Fireplace
  - ?? **2700K** — Warm Incandescent / Golden Hour
  - ?? **3200K** — Studio Tungsten / Halogen
  - ?? **4000K** — Neutral White
  - ?? **5500K** — Direct Noon Sunlight
  - ?? **6500K** — Cool Broadcast Daylight
  - ??? **8000K** — Overcast Sky
  - ?? **9500K** — Deep Arctic Cold Blue

### 2. Multi-Mode Lighting Engine
- **Key Light (Full Flood)**: Maximum surface area monitor emission for uniform, flattering lighting without harsh shadows.
- **Dual Split (Key + Fill)**: Cinema 2-point lighting right on your monitor! Set a warm key light on the left and a cool fill light on the right with custom blend angle.
- **Ring Light Mode**: Dark circular center cutout so you can look directly towards your webcam without blinding glare, creating catchlights in your eyes.
- **RGB Mood & Streamer Lights**: Full hue spectrum + presets (Cyber Pink, Neon Violet, Cyan Stream, Matrix Green, Golden Sunset, Deep Red).

### 3. Professional Studio Utilities
- **Green/Magenta Tint Correction (Duv)**: Fine-tune sensor color casts to eliminate greenish or purplish webcam artifacts.
- **Master Brightness Dimming**: 5% to 100% smooth scaling with blackout / mute switch.
- **Screen Wake Lock**: Automatically prevents your monitor from turning off or sleeping during meetings and live streams.
- **Built-in Webcam Mirror Preview**: Check how the lighting looks on your face in real-time with zero external apps needed. 100% private & client-side.
- **Auto-Hiding Glassmorphic HUD**: Controls gently fade out after 3.5 seconds of mouse inactivity, giving you 100% pure, distraction-free screen light.
